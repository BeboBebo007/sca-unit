# SCA-Unit Paid Report Public Page Review Checklist

## Purpose
Define the review checklist for the public paid SCA-Unit structural report service page.

## Clarity review
- The service description is clear
- The customer input requirements are clear
- The customer delivery format is clear
- The manual service boundary is clear
- The protected architecture boundary is clear

## Safety review
- The page warns customers not to submit passwords
- The page warns customers not to submit access tokens
- The page warns customers not to submit API keys
- The page warns customers not to submit private keys
- The page warns customers not to submit credentials or secrets
- The page warns customers not to submit unrelated private implementation details

## Limitation review
- The page does not claim legal certification
- The page does not claim financial certification
- The page does not claim medical certification
- The page does not claim cybersecurity certification
- The page does not claim compliance certification
- The page does not promise automated payment, ordering, portal access, or continuous monitoring

## Protected knowledge review
- The page references only the public SCA-Unit workflow
- The page does not expose AMNE internals
- The page does not expose protected structural architecture

## Final decision
The page should be used only after all checks pass.
