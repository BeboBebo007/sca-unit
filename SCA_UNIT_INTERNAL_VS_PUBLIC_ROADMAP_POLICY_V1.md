# SCA-Unit Internal vs Public Roadmap Policy v1.0

## Purpose
Define the difference between the internal roadmap and the public roadmap for SCA-Unit.

## Core principle
Not every internal plan should be public. Public roadmap items must be safe, accurate, limited, and aligned with the protected knowledge boundary.

## Internal roadmap
The internal roadmap may include strategic ideas, early hypotheses, experimental directions, commercial plans, protected research directions, and private invention-sensitive material.

## Public roadmap
The public roadmap may include only approved items that are safe to disclose and useful for users, contributors, customers, or public observers.

## Public roadmap allowed content
- Planned public package improvements
- Planned CLI improvements
- Planned documentation improvements
- Planned public examples
- Planned testing improvements
- Planned user-facing workflow improvements
- Approved public service improvements

## Public roadmap not allowed content
- AMNE internals
- Protected structural architecture
- Patent-sensitive mechanisms
- Private strategic roadmap items
- Internal invention directions
- Unreleased mathematical laws
- Customer private data
- Claims that imply unsupported capabilities

## Approval rule
A roadmap item may become public only if it passes product, safety, and protected-knowledge review.

## Evidence rule
Public roadmap items should be supported by real product need, customer feedback, technical evidence, or clear maintenance value.

## Scope control rule
Roadmap items that do not support the current release, current users, or current service model should remain internal until justified.

## Communication rule
The public roadmap must avoid hype and must not promise timelines that are not under control.

## Review cadence
The internal roadmap may be reviewed frequently. The public roadmap should be updated only when there is a clear and approved reason.

## Current public roadmap focus
- Improve public documentation clarity
- Improve examples
- Improve paid report workflow safety
- Prepare first controlled real customer run
- Collect evidence before expanding features
