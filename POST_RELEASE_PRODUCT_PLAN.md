# SCA-Unit Post-Release Product Plan

## Status
SCA-Unit v0.4.0 has completed its first public release phase.

## Immediate product goal
Turn public visibility into structured usage, feedback, and future paid structural reports.

## Public adoption path
- Keep the GitHub repository public and stable
- Keep PyPI installation simple
- Improve examples only when they clarify real usage
- Avoid cold outreach and external dependency
- Let discovery happen through public package, GitHub, documentation, and examples

## Paid product direction
The first paid product direction is a human-readable structural compatibility report generated from two customer-provided JSON structural states.

## Next practical focus
- Better sample reports
- Clearer paid-report workflow
- Minimal service page text
- Usage tracking through GitHub/PyPI signals

## Safety boundary
No AMNE internals or protected structural architecture exposed.

## Publishing principle
Public visibility only. No outreach. No cold messages.
