# SCA-Unit Paid Report Pricing Page Draft

## Service
Structural Compatibility Report

## Pricing principle
Simple, transparent, and suitable for early public validation.

## Basic Report
Price: 9 EUR

Includes:
- Compatibility verdict
- Compatibility score
- Basic structural findings
- Short recommendation

## Standard Report
Price: 29 EUR

Includes:
- Compatibility verdict
- Compatibility score
- Node similarity
- Edge similarity
- Shared-domain conflict signal
- Risk interpretation
- Practical recommendation

## Expert Report
Price: 79 EUR

Includes:
- Full structural interpretation
- Compatibility score and verdict
- Risk explanation
- Integration recommendation
- Priority review of structural differences
- Human-readable report for technical decision-making

## Customer input
The customer provides two structured JSON states and an optional short comparison goal.

## Service boundary
Reports are based on public SCA-Unit outputs only.

## Safety boundary
No AMNE internals or protected structural architecture exposed.

## Publishing principle
Public visibility only. No outreach. No cold messages.
