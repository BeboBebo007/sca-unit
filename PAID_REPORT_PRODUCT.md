# SCA-Unit Paid Report Product Definition

## Product name
SCA-Unit Structural Report

## Arabic name
تقرير SCA-Unit للتوافق البنيوي

## Product idea
A paid report generated from SCA-Unit results, designed for users who want a clear structural compatibility explanation instead of raw command-line output.

## What the customer provides
- Two JSON structural state files
- Or two structured logical/software states

## What the customer receives
- Compatibility score
- Verdict
- Structural differences
- Short explanation
- Practical recommendation

## First pricing model
- Basic Report: 9 EUR
- Pro Report: 29 EUR
- Expert Report: 79 EUR

## Strategic rule
The paid report must not expose AMNE internals or protected structural architecture.
