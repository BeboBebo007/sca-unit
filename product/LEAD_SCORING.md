SCA-Unit Lead Scoring Framework



Purpose



Evaluate potential pilot customers consistently before contacting them.



Scoring Categories



Technical Relevance



0 points: No clear structural comparison need

1 point: Possible but weak relevance

2 points: Clear recurring comparison or integration need



Decision Value



0 points: Assessment would not support a meaningful decision

1 point: Could provide supporting information

2 points: Could directly support compatibility, migration, audit, or integration decisions



Data Readiness



0 points: No structured technical data

1 point: Data may require preparation

2 points: Structured inputs are already available



Pilot Accessibility



0 points: No identifiable technical contact

1 point: General organization contact available

2 points: Relevant technical decision-maker identifiable



Commercial Potential



0 points: No visible budget or continuing need

1 point: Possible limited pilot

2 points: Potential for recurring or professional use



Intellectual Property Safety



0 points: Requires disclosure of protected mechanisms

1 point: Protection conditions are unclear

2 points: Pilot can operate without exposing protected source code



Total Score



0 to 4: Do not prioritize

5 to 7: Secondary lead

8 to 10: Qualified lead

11 to 12: High-priority pilot candidate



Contact Rule



Only contact leads scoring at least 8 points.



Every score must include a short written justification.
