# SCA-Unit Commercial Offer



## Product



SCA-Unit Structural Compatibility Assessment



## Customer Problem



Organizations often need to compare two system structures, configurations, architectures, or observations, but existing tools usually provide raw differences without a clear structural compatibility judgment.



## Proposed Solution



SCA-Unit accepts two structural descriptions and returns a standardized assessment containing:



- Node similarity

- Edge similarity

- Overall compatibility

- Conflict score

- Compatibility verdict

- Structured JSON report



## Initial Target Customers



- Software architecture teams

- Industrial systems teams

- Research laboratories

- Technical consulting companies

- Data and systems integration teams



## Pilot Offer



The initial pilot includes:



- Controlled access

- Assessment of selected structural files

- Structured reports

- Technical onboarding

- Limited support

- Confidential handling of customer inputs



## Delivery Model



The protected engine runs on a private server.



Customers receive access to the service and reports without receiving the protected source code.



## Pilot Pricing Hypothesis



- Private demonstration: free or invitation-based

- Small pilot project: 250 to 750 EUR

- Professional pilot: 1,000 to 3,000 EUR

- Recurring service: subscription or usage-based pricing



Final pricing requires validation with real customers.



## Current Limitations



The current version is a validated non-proprietary prototype.



It does not yet include:



- Hosted customer accounts

- Online billing

- Production authentication

- Commercial dashboard

- Protected advanced SCA-Unit mechanisms



## Commercial Goal



Obtain the first qualified pilot customer and validate:



- The customer problem

- The value of the report

- The preferred workflow

- The acceptable pricing model
