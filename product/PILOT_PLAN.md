SCA-Unit Initial Pilot Plan



Purpose



Validate whether real technical customers find structural compatibility reports useful enough to adopt or purchase the service.



Target Pilot Customers



1\. Software architecture teams

2\. Industrial automation companies

3\. Systems integration consultants

4\. Technical research laboratories

5\. Data and infrastructure teams



Pilot Scope



The pilot customer provides two approved structural JSON files.



SCA-Unit performs:



1\. Input format validation

2\. Structural comparison

3\. Compatibility scoring

4\. Conflict scoring

5\. Compatibility verdict generation

6\. Structured report delivery



Pilot Exclusions



The pilot does not provide:



1\. Protected source code

2\. Offline engine installation

3\. Internal formulas

4\. Internal decision traces

5\. AMNE mechanisms

6\. Unpublished patent material



Pilot Duration



Recommended duration: two to four weeks.



Pilot Limits



1\. Up to ten structural assessments

2\. One designated customer contact

3\. Limited technical support

4\. Customer data used only for the agreed assessment

5\. No public redistribution of reports without approval



Pilot Success Measurements



1\. The customer can prepare valid inputs

2\. The reports are understandable

3\. The results help a real technical decision

4\. The workflow saves time or reduces uncertainty

5\. The customer expresses willingness to continue or pay

6\. No protected mechanism is exposed



Initial Pilot Pricing



Private demonstration: no charge or invitation only



Small validation pilot: 250 to 750 EUR



Professional pilot: 1,000 to 3,000 EUR



Pricing remains provisional until validated with customers.



Required Feedback



1\. Which report fields were most valuable?

2\. Which fields were unclear?

3\. What decision did the report support?

4\. What integrations are required?

5\. What pricing model is preferred?

6\. Would the customer use the service again?



Current Status



The local prototype and release candidate are validated.



The hosted customer service has not yet been implemented.
