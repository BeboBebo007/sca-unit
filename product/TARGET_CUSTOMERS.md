SCA-Unit Initial Target Customers



Objective



Identify organizations and technical teams most likely to benefit from structural compatibility assessment.



Priority Customer Groups



1\. Software architecture consultancies

2\. Industrial automation companies

3\. Systems integration companies

4\. Research laboratories

5\. Infrastructure and data engineering teams

6\. Digital transformation consultancies

7\. Technical auditing companies



Customer Qualification Criteria



A potential customer should meet at least two of the following conditions:



1\. Compares system architectures or configurations

2\. Works with structured technical data

3\. Needs compatibility or migration decisions

4\. Integrates multiple systems

5\. Performs technical audits

6\. Evaluates baseline and observed system states

7\. Needs reproducible technical reports



Lead Record



For every potential customer, record:



\- Organization name

\- Country

\- Website

\- Technical field

\- Relevant department

\- Contact name

\- Contact role

\- Contact email

\- Reason for relevance

\- Proposed use case

\- Contact status

\- Response status

\- Next action



Initial Goal



Prepare a qualified list of ten potential pilot customers.



Do not contact any organization before reviewing:



1\. The relevance of the use case

2\. The confidentiality requirements

3\. The intellectual property boundary

4\. The demonstration readiness

5\. The appropriate message language
