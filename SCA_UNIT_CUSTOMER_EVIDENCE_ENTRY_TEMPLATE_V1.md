# SCA-Unit Customer Evidence Entry Template v1.0

## Purpose
Provide the official template for recording customer evidence related to SCA-Unit product, service, documentation, pricing, and workflow decisions.

## Core principle
Customer evidence must support better decisions without exposing customer secrets, private submitted data, AMNE internals, or protected structural architecture.

## Customer context
Describe the customer context only in general terms.

Do not include customer name, credentials, secrets, private file contents, or confidential implementation details.

## Evidence category
Choose one or more:
- Product
- Engineering
- Documentation
- Business
- Pricing
- Customer validation
- Service workflow
- Safety
- Protected knowledge boundary

## Evidence description
Describe what was observed, requested, misunderstood, blocked, accepted, rejected, or improved.

## Evidence strength
- Weak: single unclear signal
- Moderate: clear single signal
- Strong: repeated signal or direct customer behavior
- Critical: blocks service delivery or creates safety risk

## Impact assessment
- User impact: low / medium / high
- Service impact: low / medium / high
- Engineering impact: low / medium / high
- Documentation impact: low / medium / high
- Safety impact: low / medium / high

- No action

- Improve validation
- Improve report wording
- Improve service workflow
- Adjust pricing
- Add public example
- Add test coverage
- Keep internal for now
- Escalate for protected-knowledge review
