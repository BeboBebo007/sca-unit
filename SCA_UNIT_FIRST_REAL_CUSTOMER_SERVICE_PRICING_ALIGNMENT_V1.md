# SCA-Unit First Real Customer Service Pricing Alignment v1.0

## Purpose
Align the first customer pricing structure for the manual SCA-Unit Structural Report service.

## Pricing status
Initial controlled pricing alignment.

## Service name
SCA-Unit Structural Report

## Pricing principle
Pricing must be simple, clear, low-friction, and suitable for a controlled first customer request.

## Initial pricing tiers
### Basic Report
- Price: 9 EUR
- Scope: one structural comparison between two JSON structural state files
- Output: concise structural report

### Standard Report
- Price: 29 EUR
- Scope: one structural comparison with clearer interpretation and practical recommendation
- Output: structured report suitable for technical review

### Expert Report
- Price: 79 EUR
- Scope: one structural comparison with deeper manual interpretation
- Output: extended report with stronger explanation and follow-up notes

## Recommended first customer tier
Standard Report at 29 EUR.

## Reason
The Standard tier gives enough value to justify manual review while remaining accessible for a first customer.

## Accepted input scope
- Exactly two JSON structural state files
- Clear first-state and second-state order
- Structural comparison only

## Not included
- Legal advice
- Financial advice
- Medical advice
- Cybersecurity assurance
- Compliance certification
- Full software audit
- Handling secrets, credentials, or private keys

## Public safety rule
Do not request or store passwords, API keys, tokens, private keys, credentials, confidential secrets, sensitive personal data, or unrelated private documents.

## Protected knowledge rule
This pricing alignment must not expose AMNE internals or protected structural architecture.

## Final pricing decision
Use 29 EUR as the preferred controlled first customer price, while keeping 9 EUR and 79 EUR as optional service tiers.
