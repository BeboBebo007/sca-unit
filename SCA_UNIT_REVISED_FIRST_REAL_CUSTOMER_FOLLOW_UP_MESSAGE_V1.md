## Purpose
Provide the revised positive-language follow-up message after delivering the completed manual SCA-Unit Structural Report to the first real customer.

## Message status
Approved public-safe positive customer follow-up message template.

## Customer follow-up message

Hello,

I hope the SCA-Unit Structural Report was received successfully and was useful to review.

SCA-Unit is designed to make structural comparison between two JSON structural state files clearer, more readable, and easier to discuss.

I would appreciate brief feedback on your experience with the report.

Helpful feedback may include:

1. Was the report easy to understand?
2. Did the structural comparison help clarify the relationship between the two files?
3. Was the report format readable?
4. Was the delivery process clear?
5. Is there anything that would make the report more useful?

Your feedback helps improve the clarity, format, and customer experience of the SCA-Unit Structural Report service.

Please share only general feedback and avoid including passwords, API keys, tokens, credentials, private keys, confidential secrets, unrelated private documents, or sensitive personal data.

This report is a structural comparison report. It is not intended to replace legal, financial, medical, cybersecurity, or compliance advice.

Kind regards,
SCA-Unit
