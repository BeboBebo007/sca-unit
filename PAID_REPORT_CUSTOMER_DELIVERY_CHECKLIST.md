# SCA-Unit Paid Report Customer Delivery Checklist

## Purpose
Define what should be delivered to a customer after completing a paid SCA-Unit structural report.

## Before delivery
- Confirm that the paid Markdown report was generated successfully
- Review the report for clarity and completeness
- Confirm that no temporary internal files are included
- Confirm that no customer secrets or sensitive keys appear in the report

## Deliver to customer
- Paid Markdown report
- Optional raw SCA-Unit assessment JSON only if included in the service scope
- Short explanation of the report scope
- Clear note that the report is a structural compatibility assessment, not legal, financial, or security certification

## Do not deliver
- Temporary working folders
- Internal notes
- Test files
- Private implementation notes
- AMNE internals or protected structural architecture

## Customer note
The report reflects the submitted JSON structural states and the public SCA-Unit assessment workflow.

## Manual service boundary
Delivery is manual and does not imply automated payment, automated ordering, or continuous monitoring.

## Safety boundary
No AMNE internals or protected structural architecture exposed.
