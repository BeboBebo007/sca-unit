# SCA-Unit Product Vision v1.0

## Purpose
Define the long-term product vision for SCA-Unit as a public structural compatibility assessment tool and manual paid report service.

## Why SCA-Unit exists
Modern software and data systems often change structurally over time, but many tools focus only on text differences, schema differences, or isolated validation checks.

SCA-Unit exists to provide a practical structural assessment layer that compares two structural state representations and produces understandable compatibility outputs.

## Core problem
Users need a way to understand whether two structural representations are compatible, partially compatible, or meaningfully different in structure.

## Product vision
SCA-Unit aims to become a simple, explainable, and trustworthy structural assessment utility for developers, researchers, and technical users who need readable structural comparison results.

## Target users
- Developers comparing structural state files
- Researchers evaluating structural changes
- Technical consultants preparing reports
- Teams needing explainable compatibility outputs
- Early customers requesting manual structural reports

## Product philosophy
SCA-Unit should remain practical, transparent, testable, and limited to clear structural assessment tasks.

## What SCA-Unit does
- Compares two JSON structural state files
- Produces structural compatibility output
- Supports CLI-based workflows
- Supports readable Markdown report generation
- Supports a controlled manual paid Structural Report service

## What SCA-Unit does not claim
- It is not a legal certification tool
- It is not a financial certification tool
- It is not a medical tool
- It is not a cybersecurity certification tool
- It is not a compliance certification tool
- It does not expose AMNE internals or protected structural architecture

## Long-term direction
SCA-Unit may grow into a broader structural assessment toolkit, but only through evidence-based product decisions and real user validation.

## Success vision
SCA-Unit succeeds when users can understand structural compatibility clearly, safely, and repeatably without needing access to protected internal architecture.
